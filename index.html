How to find things quickly

Find the treatment of a disease in UpToDate
  1. Type the disease in the search box above (e.g. gout) and click UpToDate.
  2. Choose the topic that says "Treatment of…" or "Management of…".
  3. Use the Outline on the left side to jump to the part you need.
  4. For the short answer, open the section called "Summary and Recommendations" at the end of the outline.
See pictures of a skin condition
  1. Type the condition (e.g. tinea corporis) and click DermNet.
  2. Open the result. The photos are near the top. Click a photo to make it bigger.
  3. No luck? Try Google Images with the same words, adding "clinical" (e.g. tinea corporis clinical).
Find the latest official guideline
  1. Type the disease and click Find the guideline. It searches for the most recent guideline only.
  2. Prefer results from well-known bodies: WHO, NICE, ADA, ESC, AHA, IDSA, GINA, GOLD, KDIGO.
  3. Check the year on the guideline. Prefer the newest one.
Check a drug dose or interaction
  1. Type the drug name and click Drugs.com or Medscape drugs for dose and side effects.
  2. For interactions, click Interaction checker, add each drug one by one, then press Check interactions.
Useful keyboard tricks
  1. Text too small? Hold Ctrl and press +. To make it smaller: Ctrl + −.
  2. Find a word inside a long page: Ctrl + F, then type the word.
  3. Go back to the previous page: Alt + ←.
  4. Each search opens in a new tab at the top of the screen. Click the Library tab to return here.
